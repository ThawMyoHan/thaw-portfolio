About

I'm a Computer Science student at California State University, Long Beach, expecting to graduate in January 2028. I work mainly in Python, Java, C++, and SQL, and I'm comfortable moving between systems-level programming, like the shared memory and semaphore work in my Dungeon Concurrency Lab, and everyday web development with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I'm currently looking for an internship or entry-level role in software development, data analysis, or IT support, where I can keep sharpening those skills on real problems.

Outside of coursework, I've picked up a mix of technical and people-facing experience: serving as Secretary for CSULB's Badminton Club, supporting event setup and facility operations as a Student Assistant with CSULB ASI, and volunteering with organizations like the Theravada Buddhist Society of America and Mary Chapman for the Deaf. That combination taught me to stay organized, communicate clearly, and work well with people from different backgrounds, skills I try to carry into every project I build.
